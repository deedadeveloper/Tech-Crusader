A PLAIN-LANGUAGE GUIDE

WHAT WE BUILD.

Technology comes in different forms. Here's what each one is, what it can do, and how it might help you.

01

Android Applications

What is it?

Software made to run on Android phones and tablets. It can turn a device into a tool for a specific task.

What can it do?

  • Track time, inventory, or information.
  • Handle calculations and everyday tasks.
  • Provide tools for a particular business or need.
02

Web Applications

What is it?

Software you use through a web browser. Unlike a basic website, it lets you work with information and complete tasks.

What can it do?

  • Manage records, customers, or inventory.
  • Show dashboards and reports.
  • Provide online tools for customers or staff.
03

Custom Software & Tools

What is it?

A program built around a specific job or problem, instead of making you fit your work into someone else's software.

What can it do?

  • Organize or process information.
  • Perform specialized calculations.
  • Make a difficult or repetitive task easier.
04

Automation & Integrations

What is it?

Automation handles repeat tasks for you. Integrations let separate apps and services share information.

What can it do?

  • Move information between systems.
  • Handle routine steps automatically.
  • Reduce duplicate work and manual data entry.
05

Websites & Digital Experiences

What is it?

A place on the internet where people can learn about you, find information, and use online features.

What can it do?

  • Introduce a business or service.
  • Share information and answer common questions.
  • Offer forms, interactive tools, and other features.
