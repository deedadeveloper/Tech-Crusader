Bring the problem. Let's scope the solution.

Clear scope. Agreed terms.

Published amounts are starting points for smaller, well-defined work—not fixed prices for every project. Before work begins, we discuss the problem, confirm deliverables, identify dependencies, and agree on the price and timeline.

Changes to scope are discussed and approved before additional work proceeds. Hosting, infrastructure, domain registration, paid third-party services, and ongoing operating costs are separate unless expressly included in the agreement.

If you have a task, contract, project, or problem that needs solving, tell me what you're trying to accomplish. If the right solution isn't obvious yet, that conversation is part of the process.
